# PrepPilot

## Vision

PrepPilot is an AI-powered interview preparation platform that helps students and job seekers improve their interview performance through personalized mock interviews, resume analysis, and progress tracking.

The goal is to create a modern AI platform where users can upload their resume, receive tailored interview questions, practice answering them, and receive detailed feedback to improve their skills.

---

## Problem Statement

Many students struggle with technical interviews because they:

- Do not know what questions to expect.
- Have no realistic interview practice environment.
- Receive no personalized feedback.
- Cannot track their improvement over time.

PrepPilot aims to solve these problems using AI.
